Privacy Policy & Cookie Charter

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction & Philosophy

At Maison Moménto Parfumeur, we honor your privacy with the same uncompromising refinement that guides our olfactory compositions. This policy describes how we collect, safeguard, and respect your personal data across our digital boutiques and bespoke fragrance services.

2. Information We Collect

When you acquire a flacon, consult our olfactory concierge, or navigate our platform, we may collect:

  • Contact & Delivery Particulars: Recipient name, destination address, email, and telephone number for signature courier delivery.
  • Account Authentication: Encrypted credentials and username for accessing your private customer portal, order history, and saved wishlists.
  • Telemetry & Session Insights: IP address, device specifications, browser type, and interaction metrics to preserve boutique performance.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our atelier utilizes first-party cookies, session storage, and security tokens to elevate your boutique journey:

  • Strictly Essential Cookies: Indispensable for shopping bag state, CSRF protection, identity verification, and checkout completion.
  • Analytical Accords: Anonymized performance signals that illuminate navigation pathways and catalog responsiveness.
  • Personalization Tokens: Remember fragrance quiz answers, olfactory family affinities, and curated recommendations.

4. Payment Security & Gateways

Financial transactions are secured via high-grade TLS encryption and processed directly by our certified payment partner, Razorpay. Maison Moménto does not capture, store, or view full credit/debit card numbers or bank account PINs on our boutique servers.

5. Integrity & Data Sharing

We do not monetize, sell, or rent personal identities to third-party brokers. Disclosures are exclusively granted to verified operational service providers (e.g., insured logistics carriers and gateway verification engines) strictly to fulfill your order.

6. Your Sovereignty & Rights

You retain the unconditional right to access, inspect, rectify, or request the deletion of your personal records. You may alter cookie preferences anytime by clearing your browser cache or selecting Reject Non-Essential below.

7. Atelier Concierge Contact

For inquiries regarding this charter or to exercise data rights, our privacy concierge is at your service at concierge@maisonmomento.com.

Your preferences are securely stored for your boutique experience.
